## 🔬 About Me

- I am interested in the thresholds at which computational systems stop serving as instruments of prediction and begin operating as engines of further process.

- Most work remains confined to optimization within fixed evaluative frames. Of greater concern are those trajectories that refuse closure, systems whose dynamics stay open and whose principal consequence is the acceleration of subsequent intelligence rather than the polishing of what can already be measured.

- Computer Engineering background. Earlier analytical work left a preference for processes that remain generative over those that merely conclude.
